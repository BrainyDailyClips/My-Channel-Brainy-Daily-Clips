# Viral Script Template (Fill-in-the-Blank)

Based on the 13-part formula. Write in order — each section builds on the last.

---

## 1. Contradiction Hook (3–5 sec)
> "What if I told you [DESIRED OUTCOME] has nothing to do with [WHAT PEOPLE THINK CAUSES IT] and everything to do with [SURPRISING REAL CAUSE]?"

---

## 2. Quick Authority (5–8 sec)
> "I've [ACHIEVEMENT 1]. I've [ACHIEVEMENT 2]. I've [ACHIEVEMENT 3 — include a vulnerable/scared moment]. And somehow [PROOF YOU CAME THROUGH]."

Keep this to 2–3 short claims. Always pair credibility with a vulnerable detail so it doesn't read as a brag.

---

## 3. Promise Stack (5–8 sec)
> "So if you've ever wondered [PAIN POINT A], or how to [PAIN POINT B], or how to [PAIN POINT C] — here's how."

List 2–4 pain points to widen who self-identifies as the audience.

---

## 4. Debunk the Obvious Wrong Answer (30–60 sec)
> "Most people have it backwards. [X] is the output, not the input. Everyone says [COMMON ADVICE]. And listen — that's what I did too. I literally [PERSONAL ADMISSION OF TRYING THE COMMON ADVICE]. And guess what? Nothing changed."

This disarms objections before the viewer raises them, and builds relatability.

---

## 5. Core Thesis (Quotable Line)
> "[X] comes from [MECHANISM], not [WHAT PEOPLE ASSUME]."

Make this one sentence, punchy, and clip-able on its own. This is the line people screenshot.

---

## 6. Long-Form Personal Story (60–90 sec)
Structure:
- **Set stakes:** "At [AGE], I [MADE A RISKY MOVE]. I had no [RESOURCE]. I had no clue what I was doing."
- **Ticking clock:** Introduce a countdown/deadline pressure (money running out, time limit, etc.)
- **Physical specificity:** Describe a visceral bodily reaction (nausea, sweat, shaking) to make it real.
- **Turning point:** Describe the moment of success — then immediately undercut the celebration: "But none of that mattered. What mattered was [INTERNAL SHIFT]."

---

## 7. Universalizing Principle (15–30 sec)
> "Here's the thing — [X] does not discriminate. It doesn't care about your [BACKGROUND/CIRCUMSTANCE]. All it cares about is [THE REAL INPUT]."

Then introduce a second reusable mechanism, ideally something that sounds neurological/scientific:
> "Your brain [MECHANISM EXPLANATION]. That's why [CONCLUSION]."

---

## 8. Second Proof Story (30–60 sec)
A *different* personal example that reinforces the same thesis from another angle (different domain — e.g., money story then health story). Repetition with variation makes the idea stick.

---

## 9. Tactical Framework (60–90 sec)
> "If you're struggling with [X], my guess is [DIAGNOSIS — usually 'you're starting too big']. The fastest way to [GOAL] is to start with something so small you can't lose."

Give 1–2 concrete, almost absurdly small examples:
> "[NAME] didn't have them do [BIG THING]. He just had them [TINY ACTION] for [SHORT TIMEFRAME]."

Optionally include a step-by-step escalation ladder (60 sec → 120 sec → full task) to show progression.

---

## 10. Preempt the Objection
> "But I know what you're thinking — what if I'm too scared to even start the baby steps?"

---

## 11. Second Thesis / Twist (Quotable Line)
> "[EMOTION] follows [ACTION], not the other way around."

A second ownable one-liner that reframes the whole video from a slightly different angle.

---

## 12. Physiological Proof + Metaphor (30–45 sec)
Describe the actual bodily sensation of doing the scary thing, with a timeline:
> "Within [X] seconds, my [PHYSICAL SYMPTOM] starts to [CHANGE]. Within [Y] minutes, I don't feel any of it."

Then land a sticky metaphor:
> "I'm not [DESIRED TRAIT] because I don't have [OBSTACLE]. I'm [DESIRED TRAIT] because I [ACTION] despite [OBSTACLE]."

---

## 13. CTA / Content Loop
> "If you want to learn how to [NEXT TOPIC], go watch [NEXT VIDEO]."

Keep the viewer inside your content ecosystem instead of ending the session.

---

### Quick reference — the skeleton in one line each:
1. Contradiction hook
2. Brief authority + vulnerability
3. Promise stack
4. Debunk common wrong answer
5. Core thesis (quotable)
6. Dramatized personal story
7. Universalize into a principle
8. Second proof story (different domain)
9. Concrete small-step tactic
10. Preempt objection
11. Second thesis/twist (quotable)
12. Physical proof + metaphor
13. CTA loop
